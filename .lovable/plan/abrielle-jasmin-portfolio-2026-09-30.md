# Abrielle Jasmin Portfolio

## Direction
Build a single-page editorial portfolio with a cream paper-like canvas, charcoal typography, muted gray rules, and a restrained burgundy accent. The composition will feel like a polished strategy publication: strong type, asymmetrical image layouts, thin dividers, and generous whitespace rather than floating cards.

## Page structure
- Sticky compact navigation linking to Work, About, Capabilities, and Contact.
- Spacious opening with Abrielle’s name, positioning statement, education, and one selected-work link.
- Modular selected-work grid driven by a simple project data list so entries can be reordered or replaced easily.
- A larger image-first featured case study with Challenge, Approach, Creative Direction, and Deliverables.
- Four editorial principles under “Content with a reason behind it.”
- Text-led capabilities and editable tools list without ratings or fabricated proficiency.
- Brief about section with a refined photo placeholder that still looks intentional without a portrait.
- Minimal contact close with placeholder email, LinkedIn, and résumé links.

## Interactions
- Project images open in an accessible lightbox with next/previous controls for multi-image projects.
- Navigation and calls to action scroll smoothly to sections.
- Gentle entrance motion and restrained image hover treatments, disabled when reduced motion is preferred.
- Responsive layouts will preserve hierarchy and legibility on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

## Content and credibility
- Use clearly labeled concept work and editable placeholders only.
- Do not invent employers, clients, campaign results, engagement metrics, or work history.
- Add page-specific search and social metadata for Abrielle’s portfolio.

## Technical notes
- Keep project content in structured arrays near the page so future pieces, categories, descriptions, and case studies are straightforward to update.
- Use the existing design-token system for all colors and typography.
- Use original abstract editorial artwork as portfolio placeholders, avoiding generic stock imagery.
